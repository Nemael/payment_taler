On the 5th of December 2025:

You can pay invoices using Taler, but you can also make special "Taler-focused" invoices, by choosing the payment method when creating the invoices, and a Taler QR code will be written in the invoice



How does paying an invoice work

You can press "pay" on the top left, and it will create a transaction for which the Summary contains the reference to the related invoice.
Once the payment is processed
Someone on the website owner's side has to press check the payments received, and link the invoice with a payment (this is standard Odoo process)
