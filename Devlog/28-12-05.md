On the 5th of December 2025:

The cleanup is only 90% finished, but I am leaving a lot of messy debug data around, for the next steps of this addon.

I removed a lot of old unused code, and added genericity to the code. It is still in temporary state, because Odoo is not allowing regular genericity, and their Mixin system is not working on my side. I will be retrying to do Mixins, but I need to dedicate some time to it.

You can pay any invoices using Taler! And they will be marked as paid once the payment is received.

If you chose "Taler" as your preferred payment method when creating an invoice, a QR code will be added to the invoice. This QR code can be scanned to complete the Taler transaction. Please be aware that if customers use this method to pay their bill, you will have to manually reconciliate the invoice and the payment. This is standard odoo process.

Regarding fees, I took an afternoon of playing around with them, and diving deep in the documentation to understand them. Due to them being merchant-specific, and being unable to query ahead of time (I still have to confirm this), the fees might be a "surprise" to the person who's using Taler to make orders, so I am thinking of implementing a blanket fee-warning system in my add-on. I am still working on that and deciding how to proceed regarding fees.

The most useful documentation link is this one https://docs.taler.net/design-documents/012-fee-schedule-metrics.html, which cover the different kinds of fees and where they are applicable.
